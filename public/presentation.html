Problem Context & Market Need

Critical Systemic Challenges in Academic Verification

Current background checks and college admissions suffer from offline friction, fraud, and legal vulnerability.

1. Fake Degrees & Forgery
  • Over 30% of employment background checks in India encounter forged degrees or inflated marks.
  • Paper degrees with stamps cannot be authenticated offline by employers or foreign universities.
  • Proliferation of fake diploma mills damages the credibility of genuine Indian degrees.
2. Unregulated Excess Intake
  • Colleges admit students beyond AICTE approved intake caps without real-time tracking.
  • Quota violations in NRI, Foreign National, and supernumerary seats go unnoticed.
  • Cross-institution duplicate enrollments lead to seat hoarding and ghost registrations.
3. DPDP Act Privacy Violations
  • Background verification agencies scrape student data without statutory informed consent.
  • Students have zero control or audit trails over who accesses their transcripts.
  • Directly violates DPDP Act 2023 Section 6 & 7 mandates regarding purpose specification.

System Architecture & Workflow

4-Step End-to-End Auditable Verification Flow

Zero-Trust pipeline ensuring every background check is authenticated, consented, and cryptographically recorded.

STEP 01: Initiate
  • HR enters candidate 12-digit APAAR ID.
  • Picks required credential scope (Degree, Marksheet, AICTE Approval, ABC Transcript).
  • Declares legitimate purpose for DPDP statutory compliance.
STEP 02: Consent
  • Encrypted notification dispatched to student portal & mobile.
  • Student reviews requesting employer & purpose.
  • Authorizes with 1-Click Approval or Aadhaar OTP e-Sign (24h–72h token).
STEP 03: OCR Check
  • 7-Field OCR extracts Name, APAAR, Institution, CGPA, Year & Digest.
  • Cross-checked against Server Vault HMAC signature.
  • Matches 7/7 fields with 99.6% confidence score.
STEP 04: Audit Seal
  • HR seals result as 'Verified' or 'Flagged for Correction'.
  • Event fingerprinted with SHA-256 digest in immutable audit trail.
  • One-click JSON export for AICTE statutory reporting.

Innovation Spotlight · Ingestion Integrity

5 Automated Pre-Ingestion Validation Rules

Real-time gatekeeper preventing illegal, duplicated, or capacity-exceeding admissions from entering National Registry.

Rule #1: Intra-Institution Duplicate APAAR Check
Detects if the same 12-digit APAAR ID appears twice within a single college enrollment list.
Rule #2: Mandatory 12-Digit Numerical Format
Enforces strict NAD/UIDAI numerical structure, catching typos, corrupted IDs, and missing identifiers.
Rule #3: AICTE Approved Intake Cap Enforcement
Compares staged admissions against AICTE sanctioned intake plus permissible supernumerary seats.
Rule #4: Inconsistent Demographics & Nationality Verification
Validates quota categories (e.g. flags foreign nationals mistakenly enrolled under reserved SC/ST categories or age anomalies).
Rule #5: Cross-Institution Active Duplicate Detector
Flags candidates actively enrolled simultaneously in two full-time degree programs across different universities.
Statutory Privacy & Data Rights

DPDP Act 2023 Sovereign Consent Architecture

Empowering Indian students with sovereign legal ownership over their educational transcripts.

Zero-Knowledge Masking
  • Prior to student authorization, candidate name and marks remain cryptographically redacted.
  • Employers cannot fish or browse student records indiscriminately.
  • Fulfills Section 6 of DPDP Act 2023 (Notice and Purpose Specification).
Granular Scopes & Time Limit
  • Students toggle sharing permissions per document (Degree, CGPA, AICTE Seal, ABC Ledger).
  • Time-bounded tokens: access automatically expires in 24h, 72h, or 30 days.
  • Aadhaar OTP e-Sign integration creates a legally valid e-consent audit record.
Right to Withdraw (Revocation)
  • Section 6(4) compliance: Student can click 'Revoke Consent' at any point in time.
  • Immediate Effect: Verification token revoked, employer access cut off, records purged.
  • Immutable Audit Ledger logs timestamp and SHA-256 digest of revocation.
